Shipping Policy

How shipping works.

Last updated: November 2026

Ordering is open. Shipping works as follows. We confirm every order by email, usually within a day, with availability and the shipping cost.

Shipping costs

Shipping is confirmed per order and shown to you before you pay. The cost depends on the items in your order and the delivery address you enter. There are no hidden shipping charges added after we confirm your order.

Where we ship

We ship worldwide where the maker allows it. Some items have limits: MNTD. Fi Pro and the Helium Mobile Hotspots are sold for use in the US only, and the NATIX VX360 reaches Canada through authorized partners. If we cannot ship an item to your address, we tell you before you pay.

Delivery times

We do not promise a delivery date. Any delivery estimate shown when we confirm your order is an estimate from the carrier, not a guarantee. If a delivery is delayed, contact us and we will follow up with the carrier for you.

Tracking

Where the carrier provides tracking, we will share the tracking information with you.

Problems with a delivery

If a package arrives damaged, or does not arrive at all, write to contact@hoodbtc.com and we will help. See also our Returns & Refunds policy.

Your delivery address

Please make sure the delivery address you enter in your order is correct. We cannot redirect a parcel once it has shipped.
