Privacy Policy

How the HOODBTC Shop handles your information — in plain language.

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information the HOODBTC Shop collects and what we do with it. The shop is a plain storefront: it does not ask you to create an account, and it does not build a profile about you.

What we collect

  • Your cart. Your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and stays in your browser until you submit the order form. When you submit the order form, the order details are sent to HOODBTC via Netlify Forms — see “Your order” below.
  • Your emails. If you write to us at contact@hoodbtc.com, we receive the address and message you send. We use them only to reply to you.
  • Your phone number, payment choice, payment details and order notes. The order form asks for an optional phone number, your payment-method choice, the wallet address you paid from and your payment transaction hash, and any notes you add. Like your name, email and delivery address, they reach us through the order form and are used only to verify your payment, fulfil and support your order.
  • Your order. When you submit the order form, you send us your name, your email, your phone number if you gave one, your delivery address (street address, city, region, postal code and country), your payment-method choice, your payer wallet address and payment transaction hash, any order notes you add, and your order itself — the items you ordered, their variants, the quantities, the line totals and the subtotal. We use this only to confirm, fulfil and support your order.
  • Your wallet address. The order form asks for the wallet address you paid from and the payment transaction hash; those are sent to HOODBTC with your order so we can verify your payment on-chain. Separately, wallet connection is available on the home page and on the cart page. If you connect a wallet, the connection — provided by Reown (WalletConnect) — shares the wallet address you connect with your wallet and the Reown connection flow, and the page displays it (on the cart page, as the payer address). That connected address is not itself submitted with your order: on the cart page the payer-address field sits outside the order form and has no name. Connecting never asks for a payment or a signature.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not use cookies to track you. We do not run advertising that follows you around the web.

Third parties

The shop loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on Netlify. Those services may see standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the page, under their own privacy policies. When you place an order, the order information listed under “Your order” above is sent to HOODBTC through our form provider (Netlify Forms) so we can fulfil your order. If you connect a wallet, the connection is provided by Reown (WalletConnect), and the wallet modules the shop loads for that connection are served from content delivery networks — jsDelivr, with esm.sh as a fallback — which may see standard technical information when those modules load, under their own privacy policies. Payment is made in crypto (USDC) directly to the HOODBTC wallet when you order, and HOODBTC does not store your card details.

How long we keep order information, and who can see it

Order submissions can be accessed by HOODBTC and by its form provider, Netlify, which receives and stores the submission for us. We keep order information for as long as needed to handle the order, returns and disputes.

Your choices

You can empty your cart at any time on the cart page, or clear your browser's stored data to remove it completely. To ask what information we hold about you from an email exchange or from an order record, or to ask us to delete it, write to contact@hoodbtc.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is always the current one.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to contact@hoodbtc.com.
