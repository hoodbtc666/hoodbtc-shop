Privacy Policy

How the HOODBTC Shop handles your information — in plain language.

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information the HOODBTC Shop collects and what we do with it. The shop is a plain storefront: it does not ask you to create an account, and it does not build a profile about you.

What we collect

  • Your cart. Your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.
  • Your emails. If you write to us at contact@hoodbtc.com, we receive the address and message you send. We use them only to reply to you.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not use cookies to track you. We do not run advertising that follows you around the web.

Third parties

The shop loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on Netlify. Those services may see standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the page, under their own privacy policies. When you place an order, your name, email and delivery address are sent to HOODBTC through our form provider (Netlify Forms) so we can fulfil your order. Payment is arranged directly with us afterwards — by crypto or Interac e-Transfer — and HOODBTC does not store your card details.

Your choices

You can empty your cart at any time on the cart page, or clear your browser's stored data to remove it completely. To ask what information we hold about you from an email exchange, or to ask us to delete it, write to contact@hoodbtc.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is always the current one.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to contact@hoodbtc.com.
