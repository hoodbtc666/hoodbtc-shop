All sales are final

We do not accept returns — every sale is final, including if you change your mind. There is no return window, so please check the product details carefully before you order.

Faulty or damaged items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, it is covered by the manufacturer’s warranty. Write to contact@hoodbtc.com with your order details and tell us what is wrong — a photo of the problem helps. We will help you arrange a repair or a replacement through the maker under that warranty.

Some items cannot be returned to their makers: Helium Mobile Hotspots and the MNTD. Fi Pro are not eligible for returns or refunds from their makers, and the SenseCAP M1 is sold as non-returnable by Seeed. If one of those arrives faulty or damaged, the maker’s warranty is still the route — a repair or a replacement where the maker allows it. We will tell you honestly what is possible.

If we cancel your order

If we cancel an order you have already paid for — for example, if a product is out of stock or a price was listed incorrectly — you are refunded in full to your original payment method, as the Terms say. That is our cancellation, not a return.

Questions about an order

Email contact@hoodbtc.com with your order details and we will reply.
