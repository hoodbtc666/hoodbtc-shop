Returns & Refunds

How returns and refunds work.

Last updated: November 2026

Ordering is open, and this policy applies to every order.

Faulty or damaged items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, write to contact@hoodbtc.com. Tell us what is wrong and include your order details — a photo of the problem helps. We will arrange a repair, a replacement, or a refund.

Some items cannot be returned to their makers: Helium Mobile Hotspots and the MNTD. Fi Pro are not eligible for returns or refunds from their makers, and the SenseCAP M1 is sold as non-returnable by Seeed. If one of those arrives faulty or damaged, we will arrange a repair or a replacement where the maker allows it, and a refund only where we can cover it ourselves. We will tell you honestly what is possible before you decide.

Change of mind

If you change your mind about an item, contact us and we will confirm whether it can be returned and how to send it back. Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging. If you return an item because you changed your mind, you pay the return shipping.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method, once we have received and checked the returned item. A refund covers the price of the item. Shipping costs are refunded when the return is because the item was faulty or damaged. If you paid by crypto, a refund is sent in the same cryptocurrency, for the same amount of that currency that we received. If you paid by Interac e-Transfer, a refund goes back by e-Transfer to the same address.

Items we cannot take back

We cannot accept returns for items that have been used, damaged by the customer, or returned missing parts.

How to start a return

Email contact@hoodbtc.com with your order details and the reason for the return, and we will reply with the next steps.
